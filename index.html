The Legend

Why They Call Him "KPK DON"

In a world that insists on singular specialization, Praveen Kumar K rewrote the playbook by mastering three wildly different arenas.

The title "KPK DON" wasn't given lightly. It was forged on the college stage where his playback vocals left auditoriums stunned, at the drawing easel where his graphite portraits brought faces of legends like A.R. Rahman to vivid life, and at the examination hall where he walked away with top ranks and gold medals.

Praveen stands as a modern symbol for polymathy — proving that an artist can have the mathematical precision of a top scholar, and a scholar can have the sensitive, emotional soul of an artist.
